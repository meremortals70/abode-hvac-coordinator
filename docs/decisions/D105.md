# D105

An opening is an input to the room's heat balance, with a learned coupling. Free cooling is the model's own counterfactual, not a rule with a hold.

The heat balance becomes

    dT/dt = (a + v o) (T_out - T) + b (M - T) + s S + g(t) - U
    dM/dt = c (T - M) + k (T_out - M) + m S

where `o` is 1 while one of the room's openings reads open and 0 while they are shut or none is configured, and `v` is the air the opening changes with outdoors, per hour. `v` is learned like `a` and `k`, with the same log figure, its own starting value and its own process noise. Its column of the step is zero unless an opening was open during the step, so it is taught only by minutes an opening was open, and a room with no opening sensors keeps it at its starting value and never uses it. With `o = 0` the room is left with `a` alone, the leakage and glass path it always had.

So a shut room cannot be cooled by outdoor air in the model, and no hold is needed to say so. The 10-minute hold on a compressor start [D073] and the at-once stop when an opening was opened on the advice are both removed. `PlanInputs.opening` feeds the plan's own forecast, so the plan rests when the opening is open and the room is being ventilated better than the unit can cool it; the stop then waits out the minimum run like any other, which is the short-circuit guard [D016].

Free cooling advice is the same model asked a different question: the room with an opening open (`o = 1`) and the unit resting, carried forward beside the plan and priced by the same cost — comfort, energy and starts — and compared at every half-hour. It is advised when the opening moves the room's index by more than the forecast's own uncertainty and the counterfactual costs less than the plan over that window. A room whose `v` is near zero is therefore never advised to open up, because opening changes nothing for it; a room with a large `v` is. Nothing is advised while an opening is already open.

The outdoor dew point stays a measured constraint: the model has no learned moisture coupling for an opening, so outdoor air more than 1.0 C above the room's dew point is refused rather than priced (`psychro.outdoor_air_dry`). The plan's cost comparison is about heat.

On the night of 11-12 November 2026 the living room was switched to automatic control at 21:13 with its window shut and its blind down, at comfort index 31.4 against a 23.0-25.5 band. Free cooling was advised on the outdoor readings alone (22.3 C, apparent 23.9, dew point 19.5 against the room's 21.2), and the plan held its first start for ten minutes while the room sat 5.9 above its band. A double-brick room with the window shut gains nothing by waiting for air it cannot admit. That is now a fact of the model, not a rule about openings.

The advice still appears in the trace and the room's Free cooling attributes, so a window can be opened on it by day; announcements remain blocked while the room is asleep [D071].
