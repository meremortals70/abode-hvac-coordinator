# D073

When outdoor air is better, an occupied room's compressor is not started for 10 minutes, and an opening made for it stops the unit at once.

Status: superseded by [D105]: the hold and the at-once stop are removed. An opening is an input to the heat balance with a learned coupling, so a shut room cannot be cooled by outdoor air in the model and needs no rule to say so; free cooling is the model's own counterfactual. Cited in the specification and the code by `[D073]`.
