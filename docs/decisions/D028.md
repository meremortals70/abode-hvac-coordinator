# D028

Electrical draw is learned per outdoor unit, at each effort, from steps in house load. A start or stop is read from the head's mode (off or fan-only against cool, dry or heat) where the unit does not report its compressor, and the step is looked for up to 4 minutes after it (decision of 8 November 2026).

Status: in force. Cited in the specification and the code by `[D028]`.
