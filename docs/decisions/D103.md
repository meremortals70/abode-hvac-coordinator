# D103

Dry is a mode of its own, not a gentler cool. On the house's Intesis units a cool-to-dry change stops the compressor for two to four minutes: on the night of 10 November 2026 the living room was held inside its band all night while the plan swapped cool and dry 32 times, and the house load fell 430-900 W within a minute of twenty of the twenty-three cool-to-dry changes. Nothing in Home Assistant commanded a stop; the unit made it when its mode changed.

**A change of running kind is a start.** The plan charges one, as it already charges a reversal. The cycle is the run [D102], and a run is one mode. Before this rule cool and dry shared a way, so swapping them cost nothing and the optimiser alternated between two near-equal options every half hour.

**Dry is offered only where the moisture model can price it.** Its benefit is latent: the humidity filter forecasts what dry removes per hour against what cooling removes per degree. With no humidity filter there is nothing to price, and dry would be cooling at the lowest rate with an extra mode change, so the plan does not offer it. Where the filter is present the optimiser decides, against the comfort index, the energy and the start cost.

A 60 % relative-humidity threshold was added and removed on 11 November 2026 while this rule was being written: it was a rule doing the model's job, and the model already carries humidity in the comfort index [D104].

Status: in force. Cited in the code by `[D103]`.
