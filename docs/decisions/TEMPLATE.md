## Decision

One or two plain sentences. What was decided, not why.

## Why

The situation that forced it. Name the defect, the measurement or the
instruction. If a defect caused it, say what the defect did.

## Rejected

Each alternative that was considered, and the reason it lost. A rejection
needs a reason of its own - "DR-NNN forbids it" is a pointer, not a reason.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what would make it worth
revisiting.

## In the code

Checked against: build and commit. **Conforms**, **Partly** or **Does not
conform**, and one line saying why.
