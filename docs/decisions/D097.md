# D097

Rooms on one outdoor unit are one draw on the battery: each room's allowance is less what the other rooms on its unit draw now, whatever their setting.

Each room's draw now on an outdoor unit is its learned draw at the mean effort of its own running heads. The unit's draw is the sum over its rooms. The rest of the house is measured while the unit is not running, so it does not hold the other rooms on the unit; their draw comes off the room's allowance whether they are enforced, guidance or off. A room's scorecard is charged its own share.

Status: in force. Cited in the code by `[D097]`.
