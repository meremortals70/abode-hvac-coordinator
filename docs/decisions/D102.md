# D102

Never heat after a cooling cycle; never cool after a heating cycle (Jason, 8 November 2026: "there are two hard and fast thresholds. never heat after a cooling cycle and never cool after a heating cycle use pid to keep within the comfort band aiming for centre").

A hard limit, as the compressor's minimum run and rest are (Section 3). Everything else is the plan's one calculation on the learned model, with the PID holding the room on its path toward the centre of the band. The limit holds while the room is inside its band; a room that has left the band on the other side may be turned that way, so the last run of one season never blocks the next. Inside the band the room rests.
