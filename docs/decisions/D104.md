# D104

The room and the house are models; the plan optimises within them. A comfort or economic judgement is a term in the objective, not a branch in the code.

On 11 November 2026, to stop the plan alternating cool and dry, a 60 % relative-humidity threshold was added: dry above it, not below. That was a rule doing a model's job. The real defect was that a change of running kind cost nothing, though the unit stops its compressor to change mode [D103]. The threshold was removed the same day.

**What is a rule:** a fact about the equipment or the world. A unit's setpoint step and range, the modes it advertises, a compressor's minimum run and rest, a tariff boundary, a sensor that does not exist, the delay and lag of a head. These are constraints the model is built on.

**What is not a rule:** how comfortable a humidity is, when cooling is worth its energy, when a mode change is worth a start. Those are priced by the model — the comfort index carries humidity, the economics carry the tariff, `c_start` carries the cycle — and the optimiser decides.

**The test before adding a branch to `comfort.py`, `economics.py`, `limits.py`, `delivery.py` or `control/`:** is it a fact about the equipment, or a judgement about comfort or money? A judgement belongs in the model. If the model cannot express it, the model is missing a term, and adding that term is the work.

Status: in force.
