# D025

Whether the compressor ran is measured, never read from the unit's mode alone. Off or fan-only is not running. In any other mode, a unit that reports neither its frequency nor `hvac_action` is watched through the house power: a rise is its compressor starting, a fall back is it stopping (decision of November 2026).

Status: in force. Cited in the specification and the code by `[D025]`.
