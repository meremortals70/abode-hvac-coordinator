# D108

Dry is a cooling mode that removes little of the heat cooling removes.

On the living room's night of 13-14 November 2026 the unit ran 206 minutes in
dry and 364 in cool. At the same planned rate — about 1.03 °C/h, the cooling
floor — the room measured over ten-minute buckets:

| plan's first action | n | room |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cool | 36 | **−1.21 °C/h** |
| dry | 20 | **+0.65 °C/h** |

The room warmed through every long dry stretch: 03:05-04:20 +0.52 °C/h,
06:00-07:16 +0.66, 00:20-00:55 +1.47. At 00:45 the plan chose dry, forecast
the room at **24.77** in half an hour and **24.53** in an hour against a band
of 23.0-25.5, and minutes outside **0**. The room measured 25.58 and 25.78 and
crossed the top of the band at 00:50:47, staying above it for 19 minutes.

**Before this rule dry was cool.** `unit.py` lists `dry` in `COOLING_MODES` —
it does run the compressor — and `head_direction` gives a dry minute direction
**+1**, the same as cool. The heat model read direction +1 as the cooling
curve, so a dry minute was presented to the heat filter as the unit cooling at
the cooling curve's rate for its effort, and the cooling curve was allowed to
learn from those minutes. Two faults followed: the plan offered dry at the
cooling floor and forecast the room cooling at it, and the floor itself was
fitted to a mixture of minutes that cooled and minutes that did not.

**Dry has its own direction in the heat model.** A dry minute is recorded as
direction 2 and runs at `dry_share x r(effort)`, where `dry_share` is a figure
of the room learned only from dry minutes:

- its starting value is **0.1**, from the measurement above;
- the cooling curve learns only from cool minutes, so dry no longer pulls the
  floor down;
- a dry minute teaches no `U_min` (the unit's cooling floor), because what it
  delivers is not cooling;
- the plan still offers dry at the floor — that is the rate the unit is asked
  for, and `delivery.py` sends the setpoint cooling would have at it — but the
  room is forecast to cool by dry's share of it.

The figure is learned slowly, because the room's air is measured every five
minutes and the correction there absorbs most of the miss: eight hours of
alternating blocks moved it from 1.0 to 0.48 against a truth of 0.05. That is
why the starting value is the measured one rather than 1: the behaviour is
right from the first cycle, and the figure refines from there. A unit whose
dry does cool teaches it up.

Dry remains offered only where the moisture model can price its latent benefit
[D103]; this rule changes what its sensible side is worth, not whether it is
offered.

Status: in force. Cited in the code by `[D108]`.
